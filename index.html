About Salty

"You served. Don't feel guilty about claiming what you earned."

I'm a U.S. Navy veteran rated 100% Permanent & Total (P&T) who separated after my time in service.

When I first got out, I started with an overloaded VSO, submitted my records, and landed with an initial rating between 20% and 40%. Some of my service treatment records were lost, and like a lot of guys, I hesitated to push forward. I had that mindset: "Others need it more than me," and "I'm a man, I'm not going to complain about the pain or the issues I have. I'll be fine."

But as time goes on and you get older, your body and your mental health just aren't the same. The wear and tear doesn't go away.

Coming from an IT background, I realized the biggest obstacle wasn't the law—it was organizing the evidence. I taught myself how Title 38 CFR actually works, reconstructed my lost records, and used modern tech and AI to structure my claims clearly. Over time, working directly through the official VA system without paying a single dollar to unaccredited "claims consultants," I reached 100% P&T.

I created Salty & Rated to give other veterans the tools I wish I had from day one:

  • 100% Free: The calculator, AI prompts, and guides cost nothing. No paywalls, no upsells.
  • Source-Driven: Grounded strictly in Title 38 CFR and official VA.gov rate tables.
  • Completely Independent: I'm not a VSO, attorney, or accredited claims agent. I don't give individual legal or medical advice, and I never charge for claims help. For official representation, always work with an accredited VSO—their help is free.

All I ask: If any of this helps you, please like, share, and subscribe to the channels or email list. Send feedback or share your story about how this helped you. That feedback lets us optimize the website, videos, and tools so we can better serve and help our brothers and sisters navigating their own journey.
