What this is

This is the exact project-level instruction set I give Claude (Anthropic's AI) before asking it anything about VA disability claims. It tells the AI to cite 38 CFR by section number instead of guessing, to check VA.gov and eCFR before answering, to flag when sources disagree, and to never estimate a rating percentage without the criteria in front of it. It also tells it plainly where its limits are — it's not a replacement for an accredited VSO, claims agent, or attorney.
