Privacy Policy

1. The Short Version (Veteran to Veteran)

We respect your privacy. We do not sell your personal data, we do not ask for or store medical records, and we do not track individual calculations made inside our disability rating calculator. What you enter in the calculator stays entirely in your browser.

2. Calculator Data Privacy

The VA Disability Rating Calculator runs 100% on the client side (in your web browser). Ratings, body parts, and dependent options you select are never transmitted to our servers or stored in any database. Nothing leaves your device.

3. Email Subscriptions & Lead Magnets

When you voluntarily sign up to receive our Claude AI Prompt or educational updates, we collect your email address. Here is how that information is used:

  • Delivery: To send you the requested claims prompt and occasional updates, tutorials, or new tool alerts from Salty & Rated.
  • Provider: Email subscriptions are managed securely via MailerLite. Your information is never sold or shared with commercial lead-generation brokers or third-party consultants.
  • Unsubscribe Anytime: Every email sent contains an instant one-click unsubscribe link at the bottom. You can opt out at any time.

4. Web Analytics

We utilize privacy-friendly Cloudflare Web Analytics to measure site traffic and aggregate performance. Cloudflare Web Analytics does not use client-side state (such as cookies or local storage) for tracking purposes and does not collect personal identifiers or track visitors across external websites.

5. Contact Us

If you have any questions regarding this Privacy Policy or wish to request removal of your email from our list, please contact us via our official channel links or reply directly to any email received from Salty & Rated.
